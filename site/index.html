This started when I was looking at government RFPs. I saw a frequency of calls for things like water pumps and electrical transformers. Specific part numbers. So I sought out suppliers who had won these sorts of contracts in the past (public record). What I learned is that they often use eBay and large auction sites to source the equipment. Takes long hours of scouring the internet to find the thing the government proposal is calling for. AI is very good at that.Aidan

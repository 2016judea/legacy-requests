Where the prices come from. Paid is what a public agency paid per unit in a sole-source filing (price divided by the quantity it states; a contract with no quantity is listed but left out of the range). Auction is a bid on GovDeals or PublicSurplus the day it was read. Asking is a seller's price on Craigslist or a surplus dealer, not a sale. Every price links to where it was seen; a listing can sell or expire after that date. Nothing here is typed by hand.

Pictures. A photo is the seller's own listing photo. A drawing is an AI technical illustration made from the part's description so you can orient around it; it is not evidence of condition or model. 25,078 items have a photo, 0 a drawing.
